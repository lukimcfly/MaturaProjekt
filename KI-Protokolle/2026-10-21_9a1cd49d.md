## How the simulation worked

It wasn't one Python program per component. What happened:

1. **The circuit as a text file.** The AI wrote a SPICE netlist (`.cir` file). It lists every part with its value and which nodes it connects to, so it's the schematic written as text.
2. **Models for the chips.** Resistors and capacitors are simple. Op-amps like the LM324 and TL074 need a small replacement circuit (a "macromodel") that copies their datasheet behaviour: maximum gain, how fast the output can change (slew rate), and that the output can't go beyond the supply voltage. The Schmitt trigger needed its own model too. That's what "op-amp macromodels validated against datasheet" in the image means.
3. **ngspice does the calculation.** ngspice is a free circuit simulator. It ran two kinds of analysis:

Here's the signal path from left to right:

```
OSC 1 ┐
OSC 2 ├─► Mixer ─► Filter A ─► Filter B ─► Amplifier (LM386) ─► Speaker
OSC 3 ┘
```

1. **3 oscillators** (all identical). Each one has:
   - a frequency knob, about 95 Hz to 9.6 kHz. It's one double knob that tunes the sawtooth part and the other waveforms together.
   - a waveform switch: sine, triangle, square or sawtooth.
   - its own **distortion** (on/off, soft or hard, and a drive knob).
   - its own volume knob.
2. **Mixer:** combines the three oscillators into one signal, with a level knob for each channel. It's built with a transistor (BC547), not an op-amp.
3. **Filter A, then Filter B,** one after the other. Each filter has:
   - a switch for low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, or bypass (filter off),
   - a cutoff knob, about 106 Hz to 10.6 kHz,
   - a level knob.
4. **Amplifier:** an LM386 chip with the master volume knob. It's the last stage and makes the signal strong enough for the speaker.
5. **Speaker.**

On top of that, there are **Filters C and D**. They're extra filters with their own jack sockets, outside the main path. You plug them in with a cable at one oscillator's filter port, so that oscillator gets filtered on its own before the mixer.

**Power:** a ready-made ±12 V power supply, with fuses and reverse-polarity protection on the main board.

**The 3 boards:** oscillator board ×3, filter board ×4, main board ×1 (mixer, amplifier and power). All knobs and switches sit in the front panel and are connected to the boards with wires.
