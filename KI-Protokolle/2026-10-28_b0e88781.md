These come from the contract's criteria for the Arbeitsprozess:
- **Project structure:** sub-questions and sub-steps.
- **Planning of each step:** which options you considered, what you tested, and why you chose one.

- **Reflection on how you worked:** did your approach lead toward the goal?
- **Reflection on your AI use.**

The June feedback says the logbook was "sehr mager". It specifically lacked:

- **problems and how you solved them**, including mistakes
- flowcharts for any code
- **above all, reflection on the work process**
- detailed AI use ("unbedingt im Logbuch detailliert berichten!")
- and: "Logbuch verbessern und **regelmässig zeigen**!"

## Rules for declaring AI use
This is the strict part.
- **Each AI use goes in the logbook** with:
  - the tool and its version
  - the **exact prompt**
  - the date
  - **how you used the answer** (e.g. «als Inspiration verwendet», «Aufbau teilweise übernommen»)
- **The written commentary has an AI grid** with 12 areas (Themenfindung, Recherche, Hilfsmittel und Werkzeuge, Programmieren und Berechnungen, Überprüfung, Darstellung …). For each area it asks **which page of the logbook** covers that AI use. **So the logbook needs page numbers**, and the AI entries need to be easy to find.
- Undeclared or incompletely declared AI use counts as **«aktive Täuschung»**. The consequences can be having to redo the work and a disciplinary measure. Correctly declared but heavy AI use only lowers the grade for Eigenleistung.

## Formalities
There are no explicit rules for the logbook itself. A sensible baseline:
- consistent dates (you use TT/MM/JJ)
- page numbers
- clear structure
- figures with captions

The 1.5 line spacing and citation rules apply to the written commentary.
